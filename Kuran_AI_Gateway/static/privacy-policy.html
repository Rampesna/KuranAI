Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026. Applies to Kuran AI application and related services.

1. Data We Collect and Why

Kuran AI collects only the minimum data necessary to provide its services:

  • Account information: Email, name, and profile details (when signing in with Google, Apple, or email). Used for authentication and account management.
  • Profile photo: Optional. Stored on our servers solely for display in profile settings.
  • Chat messages: Your questions and AI-generated responses are stored to provide chat history. Chat messages are sent to OpenAI's API for processing (see Section 2).
  • Voice input: When using the microphone, audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. Audio is not permanently stored.
  • Location: Used only to calculate prayer times. Accessed only while the app is in use, not continuously tracked or stored. Default: Istanbul if permission denied.
  • Usage data: Daily usage counts for subscription limit management.

2. Third-Party AI Service (OpenAI)

Kuran AI uses OpenAI's API to power AI chat and voice transcription:

  • What is sent: Your chat message text (or transcribed voice input).
  • Who receives it: OpenAI, Inc. (San Francisco, CA, USA).
  • How it is used: OpenAI processes data per their Privacy Policy. API data is not used to train models.
  • No personal identifiers: We do not send your name, email, or other personal identifiers — only message content.

You will be asked for explicit consent before your first AI interaction in the app.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties except as described in Section 2.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Access to servers and databases is restricted with industry-standard security measures.

5. Account Deletion

Delete your account anytime: Profile → Security Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all data including chat history, profile, and subscription records.

6. Children's Privacy

Kuran AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or email.
